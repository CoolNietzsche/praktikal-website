How it works

Learn. Practice. Measure. Improve.

Reading a policy once a year doesn't change what people do. Practikal runs a continuous loop: short lessons, realistic practice, measured results, and follow-up training for anyone who needs it.

01

Learn

Short, focused modules on phishing, passwords, social engineering and safe data handling, grouped by topic and level.

02

Practice

Staff spot red flags in realistic emails and chats, pick the right response in scenarios, and build strong passwords.

03

Measure

Scores, pass rates and completion are tracked per person, unit, department and branch, so you can see where the risk is.

04

Improve

Anyone who falls below the pass mark gets remediation training and can retry until the behavior sticks.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Have another question? Get in touch and we'll get back to you.

How is Practikal different from a regular e-learning course?

Most courses ask people to read and then pass a quiz. Practikal has them practice: deciding whether a realistic email or chat message is phishing, working through social-engineering scenarios, building strong passwords and ordering incident-response steps. Every attempt is scored, and anyone below the pass mark gets remediation.

Can we create our own challenges?

Yes. Admins with the right permission can build challenges in all seven formats, group them by category and level, set pass marks and XP, and add translations, all without writing code.

How do we get our staff onto the platform?

Import users in bulk from a spreadsheet, or let staff register themselves and approve them in the admin console. Each person is placed in your structure (branch, department and unit), which drives campaigns and reports.

Which languages are supported?

The learner experience is available in English, Amharic and Afaan Oromo, and each challenge can be translated so the same training runs in all three.

Can we run it on our own infrastructure?

Practikal is a web application that can be hosted for you or deployed in your environment, depending on your requirements. Tell us what you need and we'll recommend an option.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Practikal runs in the browser on desktop, tablet and mobile, so frontline staff can practice from any device.
